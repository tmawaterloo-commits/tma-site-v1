Who We Are

Rooted in Faith,
Built for Campus

The Thaqalayn Muslim Association is a student-run organization dedicated to fostering a vibrant Muslim community at both the University of Waterloo and Wilfrid Laurier University. We follow the path of the two weighty things — the Holy Quran and the Ahlulbayt (as) — as our guide.

Whether you're looking for spiritual grounding, a welcoming community, or meaningful programming, TMA is your home on campus.

Join the Community
"I leave among you two weighty things: the Book of Allah and my family."
— The Holy Prophet Muhammad (s)

What We Stand For

Our Three Pillars

Spiritual Growth

Weekly halaqas, Quran circles, and lectures grounded in the school of Ahlulbayt (as), nurturing your connection with Allah.

Community

Building lasting bonds across two campuses through social events, iftars, commemorations, and a truly welcoming brotherhood and sisterhood.

Knowledge

Educational seminars, book clubs, and Islamic studies sessions that deepen understanding of our rich intellectual tradition.
